title: Reading Survey Results

Once a survey clears the [anonymity threshold](/surveys/overview#anonymity-by-default), its results unlock. DevPerform goes beyond raw averages to help you find what to actually do.

## DX Index

For DX-style surveys, responses roll up into a single trackable **DX Index** — one number you can watch over time, rather than juggling a dozen question scores.

## Per-team heatmap

The heatmap shows scores by question across teams, so you can spot at a glance which group is struggling with which dimension (e.g. one team red on "code review speed," another on "local environment"). It respects the anonymity threshold per cell — small segments stay hidden.

## Driver analysis

Driver analysis correlates survey sentiment with your **actual delivery telemetry** — connecting, for example, low "flow" scores with measured cycle-time or review-pickup data. This is what turns a survey from a vibe check into a prioritized list of what's really holding the team back, and it's unique to having both datasets in one place.

## Trends over time

Repeated sends of the same survey are tracked over time, so you can see whether an intervention moved sentiment — and whether it moved the delivery metrics alongside it.

## Anonymity, everywhere

Every results view — index, heatmap cells, driver analysis, trends — enforces the minimum-response threshold. If a segment is too small to be anonymous, its numbers are withheld rather than shown.
