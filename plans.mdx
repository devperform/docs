DevPerform comes in three tiers — **Free**, **Growth**, and **Pro**. Your plan controls which metrics and features are available; everything is gated by a single entitlement on your organization. Pricing is per engineer; see the [pricing page](https://devperform.io) for current details.

<Note>
  **New accounts start with a 14-day Pro trial** — full access to every feature, no credit card required. When the trial ends you move to the Free plan automatically (nothing is charged); upgrade any time to keep Pro.
</Note>

## How billing works

Your tier is stored on your organization. You can **upgrade from within the app** — the upgrade prompt opens hosted **Stripe checkout**, and a customer portal handles plan changes, invoices, and payment methods. Billing is per active contributor (an engineer assigned to a team); people you haven't put on a team don't count. [Get in touch](https://devperform.io) if you need help.
