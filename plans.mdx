DevPerform comes in three tiers — **Free**, **Growth**, and **Pro**. Your plan controls which metrics and features are available; everything is gated by a single entitlement on your organization. Pricing is per engineer; see the [pricing page](https://devperform.io) for current details.

<Note>
  Tier boundaries continue to evolve as features ship. The principle: **Pro** is where the AI-era and advanced/enterprise differentiators live. If you need a feature that's in a higher tier for an evaluation, get in touch.
</Note>

## How billing works

Your tier is stored on your organization. Self-serve billing (hosted checkout and a customer portal for upgrades and downgrades) is on the way; in the meantime, your plan is set by the DevPerform team when you sign up or upgrade. [Get in touch](https://devperform.io) to change tiers.
