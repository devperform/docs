<Note>
  Tier boundaries continue to evolve as features ship. The principle: **Pro** is where the AI-era and advanced/enterprise differentiators live. If you need a feature that's in a higher tier for an evaluation, get in touch.

## How billing works

Your tier is stored on your organization. When self-serve billing is enabled, upgrades and downgrades flow through a hosted checkout and customer portal; until then, your plan is set by the DevPerform team when you sign up or upgrade.
