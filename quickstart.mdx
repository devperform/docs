---
title: Quickstart
description: Go from zero to a live dashboard in about 10 minutes.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with GitHub">
    Go to [devperform.io](https://devperform.io) and click **Sign in with GitHub** to authorize the DevPerform OAuth app. When prompted, grant access to the GitHub organization whose repos you want to track. See [GitHub Org Setup](/github-org-setup) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim admin">
    The first person to sign in claims the admin role — click **Claim Admin** on the dashboard prompt. This only works once; subsequent users become regular members. If you skip it, sign out and back in and the prompt reappears until an admin exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add repos">
    In **Admin**, add the repos to track using `owner/repo` (e.g. `acme/backend`). All tracked repos are analyzed together. The first sync backfills history and runs in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your roster">
    Under **Engineer Roster**, either click **Import from GitHub org** to seed everyone at once ([details](/admin/import-members)), or add engineers manually. Each needs a name and at least one GitHub [handle](/concepts/engineers); handles can be multiple per person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize into teams (optional)">
    Under **Teams**, create a nested team hierarchy and assign engineers. The dashboard team filter scopes metrics to a team and its sub-teams.

    ```
    Engineering
    ├── Platform
    └── Product
        ├── Frontend
        └── Backend
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore">
    Open **Dashboard** for the team view (last 30 days by default). From there, explore the [DORA dashboard](/metrics/dora), [trends and flow](/metrics/flow), and the [executive summary](/dashboard/executive). Use the date picker and team dropdown to scope any view.
  </Step>
</Steps>
