The **Executive Summary** is a leadership-level view that rolls up the whole organization into a single screen — less granular than the team dashboard, designed for a VP Eng or CTO who wants the headline, not the per-engineer table.

## What's on it

- **DORA four keys** with their Elite / High / Medium / Low bands.
- **Goals on track** — how many of your [working agreements](/goals) are currently met.
- **AI-assisted work** — the share of merged PRs involving AI ([AI metrics](/metrics/ai-metrics)).
- **Investment mix** — where effort is going, plus a **week-over-week trend** of the mix ([investment](/metrics/investment)).
- **Delivery trend** — weekly PRs merged and cycle time over the last several weeks.

Each card loads independently, so a hiccup in one data source doesn't blank the whole page.

## Anomaly insights

DevPerform watches week-over-week movement on your key metrics and surfaces notable changes as plain-language insights — for example, *"cycle time is up 40% this week, driven by review pickup."* This appears as an insights banner so you don't have to go hunting through charts to notice a regression.

<Note>
  Proactive delivery of these insights to Slack and email is on the roadmap; today they surface in-app.
</Note>

## Date range

Like the rest of the app, the executive view respects the date picker, so you can frame the summary to a sprint, a month, or a quarter.
