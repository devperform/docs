title: Team Dashboard
description: Understanding the team-level metrics view.
---

The Team Dashboard is the main view in DevPerform. It shows aggregated and per-engineer metrics for your entire roster (or a filtered team) over a selected date range.

## Summary cards

The five cards at the top give a quick snapshot of the period:

- **PRs Closed** — total PRs closed across all visible engineers
- **Avg Cycle Time** — median cycle time averaged across engineers
- **Review Turnaround** — median time-to-first-review averaged across engineers
- **PRs Reviewed** — total review participation
- **Avg Merge Rate** — average merge rate across engineers

These update instantly when you change the date range or team filter.

## Per-engineer table

The table below the summary cards breaks down every metric by engineer. Click any column header to sort. Click any engineer row to drill into their [individual view](/dashboard/engineer-view).

Use the search box to filter the table by name without changing the summary cards.

## Charts

Four charts visualize the team breakdown:

- **PRs Closed vs Reviewed** — who's authoring vs reviewing
- **Cycle Time & First Review** — time-based comparison across engineers
- **Lines Changed** — output volume by engineer
- **PR Size Distribution** — stacked bar showing each engineer's size habits

Charts always reflect the team filter and date range, same as the summary cards.

## Badges

Standout performers in a period earn badges next to their name:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| ⚡ Speed Demon | Fastest cycle time on the team |
| 🔍 Thorough Reviewer | Most review comments |
| 🚀 High Output | Most PRs closed |
| 🎯 Sharp Shooter | Highest merge rate |
| 🤝 Team Player | Most PRs reviewed |

Badges are relative — they go to the top engineer in each category for the current date range. They're meant as fun recognition, not rankings.
